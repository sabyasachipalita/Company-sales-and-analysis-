# Company-sales-and-analysis-

📊 Company Sales and Profit Dashboard (Power BI)
This Power BI dashboard provides an interactive overview of company performance across various sales metrics, customer segments, and product categories.


Problem Statement:
To analyze and visualize company sales, profit, customer spending, and product category performance using an interactive Power BI dashboard. The goal is to uncover business insights, highlight high-performing regions and products, and track monthly trends for better strategic decisions.

🔍 Dashboard Highlights:
📍 Regional Insights: Profit by state (Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra lead in performance).

🛒 Category Breakdown: Clothing dominates quantity sold; Printers lead sub-category profit (₹8606).

💳 Customer & Payment Mode: Top customers by amount and payment preferences (COD most used).

📅 Monthly Trends: Sales dip mid-year but rise again in November and December.

📌 KPIs:
🧾 Total Amount: ₹438K

📦 Quantity Sold: 5615 units

💰 Total Profit: ₹37K

📐 Average Order Value (AOV): ₹121K

🛠 Tools & Techniques:
Power BI Desktop

Slicers for Quarter & State filtering

DAX for custom measures and KPIs

Clean color-coded visuals for storytelling


💡 Purpose:
To deliver a clear and insightful visualization of company performance using real-world-style sales data — enabling smarter decision-making through data.

❓ Key Questions Answered:
Which states and products generate the most profit?

Who are the top customers by revenue?

How do sales vary across months and categories?

What are the most preferred payment modes?
